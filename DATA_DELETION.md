# Data Deletion Request — AHL Radio

**App:** AHL Radio (`com.ahlradio.app`)  
**Developer:** Tritonis  
**Contact:** support@tritonis.co

---

## How to Request Deletion of Your Data

AHL Radio does not require account registration and does not store personal data on our own servers. However, we use third-party services (Google AdMob and Sentry) that may collect device identifiers and crash logs.

To request deletion of any data associated with your device:

1. **Email us** at **support@tritonis.co** with the subject line: `Data Deletion Request — AHL Radio`
2. Include your **device model** and approximate **date of use** so we can locate any associated records.
3. We will process your request and confirm deletion within **30 days**.

---

## What Data Is Deleted

| Data type | Held by | Action on request |
|-----------|---------|-------------------|
| Crash logs & device info | Sentry | Deleted from Sentry project within 30 days |
| Advertising ID & ad interactions | Google AdMob | Request forwarded to Google; Google processes per their data deletion policy |
| Favorites & recently played stations | On-device only (your phone) | Uninstalling the app deletes all local data immediately |

---

## Instant Local Deletion

All favorites, recently played stations, and app settings are stored **on your device only**. You can delete them instantly at any time by uninstalling AHL Radio from your device.
