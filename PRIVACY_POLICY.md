# Privacy Policy — AHL Radio

**Last updated: May 11, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **AHL Radio** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Android application **AHL Radio** (`com.ahlradio.app`).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
We do not require account registration. We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information directly.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
When you use AHL Radio, we may automatically collect:

- **Device information:** Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (used for ad serving and crash reporting).
- **Usage data:** Stations played, search queries entered, and in-app navigation patterns (collected in aggregate; used to improve the app).
- **Crash and performance data:** Collected via Sentry to diagnose and fix technical issues.

### 1.3 In-App Purchases
If you purchase **Remove Ads (Lifetime)**, the transaction is processed entirely by the **Google Play Billing** service. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was completed for the product ID `ahl_radio_remove_ads_lifetime`.

### 1.4 Advertising
AHL Radio displays ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect:
- Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
- Approximate location (country/region, derived from IP address)
- App usage signals for ad personalization

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device settings:  
**Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID** (Android 12+) or **Opt out of Ads Personalization** (earlier versions).

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

- Stream radio content from public radio directories (radio-browser.info)
- Display relevant advertisements
- Diagnose crashes and improve app stability
- Fulfil and verify in-app purchases
- Remember your recently played stations and preferences (stored locally on your device)

---

## 3. Data Storage

- **On-device only:** Your recently played stations, saved favourites, audio EQ settings, and sleep timer preferences are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is never transmitted to our servers.
- **Third-party services:** Crash reports are sent to Sentry. Ad data is processed by Google AdMob. Radio station metadata is fetched from [radio-browser.info](https://www.radio-browser.info) — a public, open-source radio directory.

## 5. Children's Privacy

AHL Radio is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We do not maintain a server-side user database. On-device data persists until you uninstall the app or clear app data. Crash reports retained by Sentry are subject to Sentry's own retention policies (90 days by default).

---

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:
- Access, correct, or delete data associated with you
- Opt out of personalised advertising (see Section 1.4)
- Request information about data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at **support@tritonis.co**.

## 8. Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by AHL Radio. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
